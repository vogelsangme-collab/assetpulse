AssetPulse · Nameplate OCR Bench

Test tool only — not connected to AssetPulse. Photos and text stay on this phone; nothing is uploaded. Build 2026-11-07.0843-S.

Phone check

1 · Scan the nameplate

Opens the camera with the Vogelsang nameplate outline. Line the plate up: the outline turns green, the plate is read automatically and the values are filled in — no other tap. (The round button captures manually if needed.)
Camera not working? Use a photo instead
Best results: flash off, fill the frame with the plate, shoot slightly off-straight to avoid glare. Very dark site: take the photo in the normal Camera app (Night mode) and use “Choose from library”.

2 · Line up & read

Drag the 4 amber corners onto the 4 corners of the black plate (by the screw holes). The boxes show exactly what will be read: Model & Serial (left) and Year (under the QR code).
Drag a box around the plate text. Tap once to reset to the whole photo.
Settings (for testing)

3 · Values read from the plate

Filled in automatically. Amber = check this one against the plate; blank = type it. Edit any value directly. Nothing is saved until the asset is saved.

4 · Simulated AssetPulse form

Shows how values would land in the app: blank fields are filled; a field that already has a value is only replaced if you agree. Type something in a field first to test that rule.
